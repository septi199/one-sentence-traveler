# Notes — One Sentence for a Traveler from 1800

- Bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/u-po-pu-one-sentence-for-a-traveler-from-1800-7e6162fb
- Prize: $2 across 10 places. Deadline: Wed 25 Nov 2026 02:25 WIB. Three blind rounds.
- The sentence (180 chars): "Imagine every book, letter, and song in the world pressed into a single glass thread, so that any man in any town may read, hear, and answer them all in the time it takes to blink."
- The hard rule is vocabulary: no word coined after 1800. Every word here (book, letter, song, glass, thread, town, blink) predates 1800, and the sentence paints one picture instead of listing features.
